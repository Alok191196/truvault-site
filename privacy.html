TruVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app in.gamezalgames.truvault

Short version: TruVault has no servers, no accounts and no analytics of its own, and we never receive your data. Everything you save stays encrypted on your device. One exception to know about: the card scanner uses Google’s ML Kit text-recognition component, which reports anonymous diagnostic and usage data to Google and is the reason the app carries the INTERNET permission. Your vault entries and the images you scan are never part of that.

1. Who we are

TruVault is an offline password manager for Android published by Alok Kumar Singh. Contact: alokdevelopment19@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, transmit, sell or share any personal data. We operate no backend service that the app talks to. We have no ability to read your vault, and no way to recover it for you.

The app is not entirely free of network activity, however. Google’s ML Kit text-recognition component, which powers card scanning, sends Google diagnostic and usage data about itself, such as device and SDK information. That data goes to Google, not to us, and it never contains your vault entries, your card details or the camera images. See section 7.

3. Data the app stores on your device

  • Vault entries you create: titles, usernames, passwords, URLs and notes.
  • App preferences, such as your screenshot-protection and auto-lock settings.
  • Card details you scan or type, which are stored exactly like any other entry. Scanned images themselves are never stored.

This data is written only to TruVault's private app storage on your device. No other app can read it, and it is never uploaded.

4. How your data is protected

  • Each entry's sensitive fields are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a unique initialisation vector.
  • The database itself is encrypted at rest with SQLCipher.
  • Encryption keys are held in the Android Keystore, hardware-backed on devices that support it. Keys never leave the device and are not extractable by the app.
  • Opening the vault and revealing a stored password require biometric or device-lock authentication.
  • Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app by default (FLAG_SECURE). You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Android system backup is disabled for this app, so your vault is not copied into Google Drive backups.

5. Permissions we request

  • USE_BIOMETRIC and USE_FINGERPRINT — to unlock the vault and to confirm it is you before a password is revealed. Biometric data is handled entirely by the Android system; TruVault never sees your fingerprint or face data.
  • CAMERA — only so you can scan a bank card instead of typing its details. The camera opens only when you tap “Scan card”. Frames are read in memory on your device, are never written to storage and are never transmitted. The text recognition model is built into the app, so scanning works with no internet connection. You can always type card details in by hand and never grant this permission.

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are present in the app because the ML Kit component adds them. TruVault’s own code makes no network requests, and your vault is never sent anywhere. TruVault requests no location, contacts, microphone or storage permissions.

6. Export and import

You can export an encrypted backup of your vault to a file of your choosing. Once that file leaves the app it is under your control, and its safety depends on where you put it. Keep exports somewhere you trust, and protect the passphrase you used.

7. Third parties

TruVault contains no advertising SDKs and no crash-reporting SDKs, and we run no analytics of our own.

The app does include one Google component: ML Kit text recognition, used only to read a bank card you choose to scan. The recognition model is bundled in the app and runs offline, so your card images are processed on your device and are never uploaded. The component does, however, report diagnostic and usage data about itself to Google — device and SDK information of the kind described in Google’s ML Kit data disclosure. Google has published no supported way to switch this off. That data is handled under Google’s privacy policy, and it contains no vault entries, no card details and no images.

If you install TruVault from Google Play, Google also collects installation and billing information under its own privacy policy; that is between you and Google, and TruVault receives none of it.

8. Children

TruVault is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

9. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data from Android Settings, permanently destroys the vault on that device. There is no recovery. Export a backup first if you want to keep your entries.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
